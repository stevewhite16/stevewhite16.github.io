Reading the picture. The blue curve is the sampling distribution of x̄T − x̄C if the candidate were the true difference; the gray tails are its rejection region at the chosen α. If the black line (what you actually observed) lands in a shaded tail, that candidate is rejected. Larger n or smaller s shrinks the SE, narrows the curve, and shortens the blue band. Practice #2 and #3 use one pooled SD of 5.5 (the slides use 6 and 5), so SE is within 0.01 of the slide values; the TMS preset uses SD ≈ 11 and n ≈ 51, which lands near the paper’s −4.2 to 3.3.
